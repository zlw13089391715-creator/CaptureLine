CaptureLine is an independently maintained derivative of [Tendedero](https://github.com/alejandrobujan/tendedero), created by **Alejandro Buján**. The clothesline concept and original implementation come from Tendedero.

The source baseline is upstream commit [`ed0618a67cc59e0a8621b97bb5e32ab9af3b0b69`](https://github.com/alejandrobujan/tendedero/commit/ed0618a67cc59e0a8621b97bb5e32ab9af3b0b69).
